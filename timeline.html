2 November 2021 🌱

The First Time I Saw You

Do you remember that day?

It was 2nd November 2021, when we started our BS. I saw you for the first time that day. There wasn't anything particularly special happening, but somehow I was naturally drawn towards you. ✨

You were sitting two seats ahead of me, and at some point you turned around to ask something. And that's when I saw your eyes. 👀

And there you go. 😂

Later, I found out they were lenses. Hahaha. 😂 But at that moment, I didn't know that. I just remember looking at you and thinking there was something about you that caught my attention.

After that, I kept trying to find a chance to talk to you. The problem was, I'm a little introverted, so somehow I never really got the opportunity. 😅

And then there was our class schedule. We only had classes for three days that week. I used to come for the first three days, while you came for the last three.

So just like that, those first few days passed... 🌌

♡ ✦ ✨ ✧ 🤍
A Little While Later ✦

And Then I Saw you Again

After those first few days, I didn't really get another chance to talk to you, or even see you.

Then our class groups were formed, and almost a week later, I saw you again in class. And that was when I found out that you were our class CR.

I was with Hina that day, and when I saw you, I got so excited and said, “Wo larki mujhe dobara nazar aa gayi hai.” 😂

After that, we had a little hi-hello introduction, and then... silence again.

We went back to being just class fellows.

❤️ ✦ ♡ 💫 ✧
Somewhere Along the Way ✦

From Classmates to Friends

After that, we slowly started talking to each other. Nothing too personal at first — mostly just class-related things, because our classes were still online.

And then there was that whole situation with a class fellow. I'm not going to mention the name here, of course, but you know exactly who I'm talking about. 😂

Somehow, that situation brought us closer. We started talking more, and somewhere along the way, we became proper friends.

By the time we became proper friends, we had already started knowing each other quite well. And I still remember that we met on 10 June.

But then came 2 August. ❤️

It was our first semester's first paper, and I remember how incredibly excited I was to finally meet you. I honestly don't think I have enough words to explain how excited I was that day.

And the best part was that we were both excited to see each other. That day still has a very special place in my memory. ✨

✦ 😂 ♡ ✨ ✧
Somewhere Between the Laughs ✦

The Days We Started Becoming Us

And then came the time when we started spending more and more time together. Teasing each other, taking panges, creating dramas for absolutely no reason — somehow, that became our thing. 😂

But there is one thing I will always give your friendship credit for: the confidence you helped build in me.

You were the class CR, so somehow no work of yours was ever complete until you had managed to involve me in it somehow. And honestly, I wouldn't have it any other way.

I still remember that introduction with Ma'am Saba. I went there, gave my little introduction — whatever it was — while you just stood there looking at my face like, “What exactly are you doing?” 😂

I don't know if Ma'am Saba remembers that introduction, but I definitely do. And somehow, those silly little moments became some of my favourite memories of that time.

The teasing, the dramas, the panges — we never really stopped. And somewhere between all of that, your friendship was quietly becoming something very important to me.

🌱 ✦ ♡ ✨ ✧
One Morning ✦

The Gobhi Paratha

And then there was that one morning I still remember so clearly.

You were hungry early in the morning, and somehow I ended up bringing you a gobhi paratha. 😂

We sat in class and ate it together, and honestly, it was such a simple little moment. Nothing extraordinary was happening, but somehow these were the moments that made our friendship feel more and more special.

After that, slowly and quietly, our bonding kept getting stronger. I started becoming concerned about everything happening in your life, and without even realizing it, you had become someone genuinely important to me.

Looking back, it is funny how something as simple as a gobhi paratha can become a memory you remember years later. 🌱

☁️ ✦ 🤍 ✧ ♡
The First Fight ✦

The First Little Storm

Our first fight was over something so small that, looking back now, I almost laugh at how big it felt to me back then.

But at that time, it wasn't small for me at all. It was our first real fight, and I know I overreacted. I agree — I was never the most emotionally mature person. 😅

I remember feeling like something was being taken away from me, like a part of me was slowly moving away, and I didn't know how to handle that feeling.

And then came that message from you — telling me that if I tried to talk to you at college the next morning, you wouldn't come to college again.

I was literally sitting there eating roti when I read it and thought, “What? Seriously?” 😂

Of course, that wasn't the end of us. We eventually made up — and yes, I know you were the one who took the first step. ❤️

We've had plenty of fights since then, and I'm sure we haven't seen the last of them. But somehow, every fight became another part of our story.

Looking back, I think that first fight taught me something: sometimes, when someone becomes really important to you, even the smallest distance can feel much bigger than it actually is.

🤍 ♡ ✦ ✨ 🫶
A Moment I'll Always Remember 🤍

The Hug That Fixed Everything

Then came the time when we went for training at school for the first time. And honestly, we spent that whole time laughing, joking, eating, and just being ourselves. 😂

Of course, we also managed to have one of our little fights. Because apparently, we couldn't spend too much time together without finding something to fight about. 😂

But after that little fight, you came upstairs and I was standing there. And I just hugged you.

I hugged you and didn't want to let go. And you were like, “Han theek hai larty raha karo bus muja sa.”

And then you hugged me back.

And just like that, we sorted everything out.

That was one of the best moments.

✨ 🎀 ♡ ✦ 💫
Somewhere Along the Way ✨

The Little Moments That Became Memories

And then there were our lunches, our parties, our birthday plans, and all those little moments in between.

We never really made it to dinners, so let's just call them lunches. 😂 But honestly, the time I spent with you was amazing.

There are so many moments from those days that I could never possibly fit all of them into this journey.

And then, of course, you got married and didn't even invite me. 😭😂

I had literally made all the preparations. How was I supposed to know that you were going to get married so quickly?

But anyway, it's okay. I'm happy for you. 🤍

Looking back, it wasn't just the big moments that mattered. It was all those little things we did together that slowly became memories.

✦ 🤍 ✨ 💫 ♡
Six Years Later ✨

Six Years Later...

And just like that, six years have passed.

Six years filled with different turns, different situations, different versions of us, and more memories than I could ever fit into these pages.

We had our good days. We had our difficult days. We fought, we misunderstood each other, we got upset, and sometimes we probably wondered what was happening to us.

But if we are still here today, after everything, then there must have been something really beautiful in all those years too.

I couldn't possibly put every memory, every conversation, every laugh, every fight, and every little moment into this journey.

I just wanted to take you back to where it all began and remind you of how far we've come.

So this is where our little memory journey ends... but of course, our story doesn't. 🤍

🌿 🌱 🌿 🌱 🌿 ♡ 🤍 ♡ 💗 ♡

And Then...

Six years of memories, laughter, little fights, endless conversations, and moments that became a beautiful part of our story.

And now, there's one more memory I want to create with you.
